How should the mAbV work be told as a portfolio case study?

I went through everything on mAbV: the vault records, the original repo on Drive, the live site, the office photos and the three reference sites. This page holds the plan, plus nine questions for you.

Verdict: a strong 10-slide story, with the office signs in Guangzhou as the proof.

The spine is simple: an antibody becomes a shield, then a system, then a kit, then a sign on a real door. Every step has a dated record.

One thing changes the headline. The final mark closely matches "Jin's Design", a redraw of Jin's own sketch in the April logo comparison. So the deck can't say "I designed the mark" until you tell me how the credit splits (question 2).

Nothing is built yet, and the oganiko.com repo is untouched. For each question, approve my default or type your answer in the note. Phase 2 starts once your answers come back through First Mate.

1. Art direction and slide plan

Clinical calm, one blue, the work at full size. From Metalab: a large project name, a short metadata row, then one idea per slide on a big, careful crop. From Brittany Chiang: a plain first-person voice that says exactly what I did. The frame is OGANIKO's and stays the same for every future case study: Open Sans, black on white, square corners, hairlines. The colour comes from the work: cobalt #0047BB, navy and slate on white. Marks come from the vector files, never redrawn or recoloured. From Robby Leonardi, only the principle: the deck shows instead of telling. The mark renders live from 16 to 96 px, and the colour slide sets the real spec.
