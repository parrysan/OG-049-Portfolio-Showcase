mAbV, step by step

From a founder's sketch to a sign on the door.

  1. A young company that had said little in public yet, working with Oxford University. It came with a working name, a domain and a paragraph of science, and needed an identity investors and scientists could trust.
    mmonoclonal
    Abantibody
    Vvaccine

    Every letter is intentional. The casing makes the acronym readable inside the word.

  2. An antibody is shaped like a Y, and the mark is built from one. The shield around it frames what the company works on: protection before infection.

    Four bars: the antibody's two arms form the V

    The shield: protection before infection

    A teardrop stem forms the point

  3. I developed the mark from the founder's first sketch into the final system, testing it against ten other routes at three sizes and on navy.

    Five of the eleven routes, converging on the final mark.

  4. Teal is the category's default, and the first draft's teal sat close to a familiar financial brand. I moved mAbV to one cobalt, tied to Pantone 2728 C so print matches screen.
    First draft: teal, 2.6 : 1 on whitePantone2728 CCobalt Blue

  5. In China the company trades as 百卫信, set in Noto Sans SC Bold to match the DM Sans wordmark, and outlined so it looks the same on any device.

    DM Sans Bold for Latin · Noto Sans SC Bold for Chinese

  6. Eight sections, live at mabvbio.com. The rules are shown, not just stated: below 32 px the mark switches to a simple V.
  7. Eighteen files in SVG, PNG, PDF and EPS, outlined type, the Pantone instruction for printers, and a readme in English and Chinese.
    • Digital4 SVG · 4 PNG
    • Print4 PDF · 4 EPS
    • Social1 SVG · 1 PNG
    • ReadmeEnglish · Chinese
  8. The identity on the company's doors in Guangzhou: a reception sign and frosted film on the glass. A real installation, not a mockup.
